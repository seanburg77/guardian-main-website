Good to Know

Frequently asked questions.

Have a question that isn’t covered here? Get in touch and we’ll be happy to talk it through.

Do you clean both homes and businesses?

Yes. Guardian is focused on residential and commercial cleaning. Tell us about your property and the type of service you’re looking for.

Can I set up recurring cleaning?

Yes. We can discuss recurring service and a schedule that fits your property and priorities.

Do you offer deep cleans and move-out cleans?

Yes. Deep cleaning and move-in or move-out cleaning are among the services you can request an estimate for.

How do you price a cleaning?

Pricing depends on factors such as property size, condition, service type, requested tasks, and frequency. We’ll discuss the scope before providing an estimate.

What areas do you serve?

Guardian is based in the Buffalo and Western New York area. Contact us with your property location so we can confirm service availability.
